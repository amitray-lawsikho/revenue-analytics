# Definitions (fixed rules)

Change these only with Amit's explicit OK, and change `etl/rules.py` / `etl/refresh.py` in the same commit.

| Term | Rule |
|---|---|
| Revenue | Sum of `Fee paid`, every payment row (all Enrollment types, incl. "Other revenue" and blank) |
| New enrolment | A row whose `Enrollment` is exactly "New enrollment" (case and spaces ignored) **and** whose `Fee paid` is above ₹800 (₹800 or less adds revenue only; Amit, 26 Oct 2026, was ₹1,500). "New enrollment - balance payment" and all balance/collection rows add revenue only |
| Scope | All payments of the organisation (no vertical filter) |
| Current caller | Revenue "Caller name" matches a `Teamlist` "Caller Name" exactly (case, dots and spaces ignored) or a `name_alias` row. Credited to the caller's **current** Teamlist team and vertical, for all history |
| Teams by month (from 1 Jan 2025) | Amit, 12 Nov 2026: a payment dated 1 Jan 2025 or later by a current or resigned caller takes the caller's team and vertical from `MonthwiseTeamList` (same sheet as Teamlist) for the payment's month (column "Month" = first date of the month). The caller is found by email (current callers), else by name (case, dots and spaces ignored). Not listed that month: the nearest month where they are listed (the earlier one on a tie). Never listed: the rules above and below. Rows without a vertical are ignored. Old vertical names are mapped to today's (Uzair-Elite, Uzair - Bootcamp/Test Prep/Jyoti/Anas team, Uzair Ahmad Khan, Team Sharique, Team Sana → Team Uzair; Anmol Gakhar, Priya Singh, Team Aishwar → Team Anmol; Team Mayur (NCLEX), Team Ankur → Team Mayur; Team Tathagat, Team Shivya, Team Aastha, Team Dhruv, Team Dhruv & Shivya, Team ID → Team Darshan; Team Karman Kaur → Team Karman; Shalu Jha → Team Shalu; Community → Team Abhipsha; Deepanshi → Team Deepanshi; `etl/config.py` VERTICAL_RENAMES); every other name (Data Science Vertical, trainee batches) is kept as written; team names are kept as written. Payments before 2025 keep the current Teamlist. Direct and blank callers keep their rules |
| Revenue type | **Calling** = Enrollment "New enrollment" or "New enrollment - balance payment". **Collection** = "Bootcamp collections - balance payments" or "Community collections - balance payments". **Other** = everything else ("Other revenue", blank, older labels such as "Bootcamp caller - balance payments", "Subsequent installment dig", "2nd installment"). Calling + Collection + Other = Revenue |
| Direct | Caller name containing the word "Direct" ("Direct", "Bootcamp - Direct", "Bootcamp - Direct-International"): direct payments, not callers; each name is shown as a caller of its own, under the team/vertical written on the sheet row. Overall shows them together in one "Direct payments" card |
| Bootcamp joining fees | "Combined Bootcamp Joining Revenue Sheet 2026-2027", tab Combined (it combines the monthly tabs of "Bootcamp Joining 2026 - Revised"). Stored as daily totals per course, credited to "Bootcamp Joining - Direct" (vertical and team "Bootcamp Joining"). Revenue only, never an enrolment. Dates from 1 Jul 2026 |
| IIT | A payment whose course name contains "IIT" or "iHub" (any case; "i-hub", "i hub" too). IIT revenue = all such payments. **IIT sold** (Amit, 30 Oct 2026) = unique customers per IIT course among the IIT payments in the chosen dates, any Enrollment type (new or balance); a customer is the same phone (last 10 digits), else the same email; a customer counts in every period with a payment and for each caller who took a payment (team/vertical totals add up the callers); a row with neither phone nor email counts as its own customer; bootcamp joining daily totals never count. Phone/email are read for this only and kept as a keyed one-way code (`revenue_txn.cust_key`), never stored. IIT new enrolment = a new enrolment (rule above) with an IIT course (no longer shown). IIT balance payment = a "New enrollment - balance payment" with an IIT course (upgrades where the first payment's course had no IIT). Collection teams = Team Mayur and Team Anmol; all other verticals are sales teams |
| Realtime Tracker match (Leads Console) | Amit, 30 Oct 2026: every "New enrollment" of ₹999 or more gets two keyed one-way codes, phone (last 10 digits) and email (`revenue_txn.ph_key`, `em_key`), made the same way as the IIT code. The Leads Console codes an uploaded lead's phones/email the same way and `lc_rev_match` (service role only) returns the caller of the latest matching payment; that lead is shown as "Actually Enrolled" under that caller. Phone/email are never stored |
| Student Dictionary | Amit, 17 Nov 2026. **Rows**: every "New enrollment" and every balance / instalment payment (Enrollment containing "balance" or "installment"), all years (Apr 2023 on); "Other revenue", blank and bootcamp joining daily totals are not used. **Student** = the same phone (last 10 digits) or the same email (lower case), chained: phone A with email X and email X with phone B are one student; a phone used with more than 5 different emails, or an email used with more than 5 different phones, does not join students (office / dummy contacts). A row with neither is its own student. **Course** = the same course name (case and spaces ignored). **Courses enrolled** = courses with a New enrollment row. **Sales price** = `Course Price` on the course's New enrollment row (the highest if there are several). **Paid** = `Total fees paid (for Ops)` (not `Fee paid`) of the New enrollment + balance rows of that course; `Fee paid` when Total fees paid is blank. **Status**: Matches (paid within ₹1 of the price), Balance pending (paid less), Paid more (paid more), No price (Course Price blank or 0), Balance only (balance rows whose course name has no New enrollment for that student: usually a course spelled differently). The page lists students whose course enrolment date (first New enrollment, else first payment) is in the chosen dates and vertical / team / caller (the enrolling caller's). The student's main phone and email are the ones on their most recent payment; any others are shown as alternate phone / alternate email (each with a copy button). Name, phone and email are kept only for these rows, in private tables (`rev_student_txn`, `rev_student`, `rev_student_course`; no direct read), and shown only to emails listed in `allowed_users` |
| Lead details not available | Blank caller name (or NA, -, nil): under the sheet row's team/vertical, else "Not assigned" |
| Resigned | Any other caller name (not in Teamlist): under the sheet row's team/vertical; the past FY tabs have no team column, so "Resigned – team unknown" |
| Source of rows | Dates from 1 Apr 2026: `Combined` sheet (+ bootcamp joining daily totals from 1 Jul 2026) (reloaded every run, so back-dated entries simply appear). Earlier dates: Consolidated FY tabs (reloaded in the 09:00 run). No date is read from both |
| FY 2023-24 caller | That tab has two caller columns; "Caller name for Sales" is used |
| Comparison | Set by the date button (Amit, 26 Oct 2026). Today → yesterday; Yesterday → the day before; This week → the same days of last week; Last week → the week before; This month → the same days of last month (e.g. 1–26 Oct vs 1–26 Sep); Last month → the month before; This FY → the same dates of last FY; Last FY → the FY before; All years → no comparison. A chosen financial year or custom From/To dates → the same dates one year earlier. The page always says which dates |
| Part / Month / Year | Part of month = days 1–10, 11–20, 21–end; year = Indian financial year (Apr–Mar). All dates IST |
| Refresh | 09:00 (full), 13:00, 17:00, 20:00, 23:00 IST via Supabase pg_cron, plus the dashboard's Update button |

Calling (hidden from the dashboard for now): daily summaries keep being saved; BigQuery keeps ~60 days, Supabase keeps them.
